# PROBLEM

Buying model access is the easy part. Here's where companies get stuck:

- **No workflow ownership.** Generic AI tools sit at the edge of your operations. They answer questions. They don't close tickets, process invoices, or move deals forward — because nobody mapped the business objects they'd need to act on.
- **No accountability layer.** When an agent makes a decision, who audited it? Without eval frameworks and audit trails, AI in production is a liability, not an asset — especially under PE scrutiny.
- **No one to operate it.** Models deprecate. Workflows change. New use cases emerge. Without an implementation fabric that's maintained and iterated, every deployment becomes technical debt by month three.
